Our Strategy

At VitaGlow, we've charted a clear path to becoming a benchmark for sustainable beauty in Colombia, connecting deeply with Generation Z and Millennials.

Main Objective

Position VitaGlow as the leading sustainable beauty brand among Generation Z and Millennials in Colombia within 12 months.

Key Activities

  • Launch our intuitive and engaging e-commerce platform.
  • Develop influential social media campaigns with content creators aligned with our values.
  • Launch a line of innovative, high-quality eco-friendly products.
  • Implement loyalty and referral programs to build a strong community.
  • Establish alliances with organizations focused on sustainability.

Schedule

Our plan spans 12 months, with quarterly milestones to ensure progress and continued adaptation.

Success Indicators

  • Monthly sales growth.
  • Increased web traffic and conversion rates.
  • Increase in social media engagement and number of followers.
  • High customer retention rate.

Critical Success Factors

  • Constant quality and innovation in our products.
  • Effective and authentic digital marketing.
  • Exceptional customer experience at every touchpoint.

Potential Risks

  • Low initial market adoption.
  • Supply chain disruptions.
  • Negative customer reviews.

Mitigation Measures

  • Conduct ongoing market research and pilot testing.
  • Diversify suppliers and establish proactive and efficient customer support.
