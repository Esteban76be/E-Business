Online Visibility: SEO and SEM

To ensure you can easily find VitaGlow and connect with our philosophy, we implement SEO and SEM strategies focused on Colombia’s young, eco-conscious audience.

Strategic Keywords

We focus on terms that reflect your searches and our values: sustainable beauty, natural skincare, eco-friendly cosmetics, vegan products, skincare Colombia, conscious beauty, eco-friendly makeup.

SEO Actions (On-Page)

  • Optimization of titles and meta descriptions with our keywords.
  • Creation of a blog with practical tips on sustainable beauty and wellness.
  • Use of compressed and properly labeled images for fast and accessible loading.
  • Development of a responsive, intuitive, and user-friendly web structure for all devices.
  • Detailed, transparent, and optimized product pages for easy understanding.

SEM Strategies

  • Targeted campaigns on Google Ads and Meta Ads (Instagram/Facebook).
  • Segmentation by key interests: sustainability, natural beauty, veganism, conscious personal care.
  • Remarketing ads to reconnect with interested visitors to our site.
  • Use of engaging visual formats and short videos to capture attention and convey our message.
